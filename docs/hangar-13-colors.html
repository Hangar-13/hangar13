Hangar 13 colors

Current site colors: paper, ink, orange, and blue. The previous yellow and navy palette is not included.

Brand

ColorHexWhere it is used
#ff4d00 Orange. Primary buttons, sidebar mark, charts.
#e64500 Orange hover. Sign-in and sign-up buttons.
#0055ff Blue. Links, focus rings, secondary actions, section labels.
#121417 Ink. Body text, dark bands, footer, and the light-mode sidebar.
#f4f7f9 Paper. Page background in light mode, and body text in dark mode.

Surfaces and text

ColorHexWhere it is used
#ffffff White. Cards, menus, form fields, and text on orange, blue, or ink.
#e8eef2 Mist. Quiet fills behind secondary content in the app.
#e9edf0 Cloud. Marketing section fill and the secondary button hover.
#515860 Slate. Secondary text and supporting copy.
#30363d Graphite. Lead paragraphs on the marketing pages.
#a3a9af Ash. “Not included” marks in the comparison table.

Dark mode

ColorHexWhere it is used
#121417 Page background. Ink from the light theme becomes the canvas.
#1a1d21 Charcoal. Cards, menus, and form fields.
#0d0f12 Night. Sidebar, one step darker than the page.
#2a2e33 Panel. Quiet fills behind secondary content.
#9aa3ad Fog. Secondary text.
#f4f7f9 Paper. Body text on the dark page.

Status

ColorHexWhere it is used
#dc2626 Red. Destructive actions in light mode.
#f87171 Coral. Destructive actions in dark mode.
#b42318 Crimson. Error text on the mailing-list form.

Tints

These are the solid colors above, used at a percentage. They are borders, captions, and focus rings, not separate paints.

ColorMixWhere it is used
#121417 at 10–25% Borders and dividers. 40% and 45% are faint labels. 50% and 70% are photo overlays and the mailing-list scrim.
#ffffff at 65–70% Captions and secondary lines on ink backgrounds.
#0055ff at 25% and 40% Focus rings on fields and the mailing-list checkbox.
